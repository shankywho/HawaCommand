Hardware

The sensors, and what each one costs

Low-cost sensors ride on vehicles already moving through the city. For the demo, a simulator emits the exact same MQTT packets, so a real pod plugs in with no cloud change.

Scroll to take it apart
HawaPod-M
₹7,000–9,500

Rides on buses, e-rickshaws and delivery bikes. Street-level PM, gases, weather and GPS. A tiny on-device model flags spikes and publishes them instantly.

{{ p.k }}{{ p.v }}
HawaPod-S
₹4,500 + ₹2,500 CO₂

A school pair: gate and classroom. Indoor unit adds an NDIR CO₂ sensor, so the school knows when to seal windows and when to ventilate.

HawaTag
≈ ₹6,000

Fits sprinklers and smog guns. Water-flow and pump-current sensors prove spraying happened, not just driving. Cab-roof PM sensor captures before and after.
